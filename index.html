This Privacy Policy explains how BookMyPhone ("BookMyPhone", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and handles information when you use the BookMyPhone mobile application and related services.

BookMyPhone is a marketplace platform designed to help customers discover pre-owned mobile phones offered by sellers, make bookings, visit seller stores, and communicate with sellers or support where applicable.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use BookMyPhone, we may process information such as:

  • Name and email address.
  • Account and profile information.
  • Authentication and account identifiers.
  • Seller profile and store information.
  • Seller verification or KYC information where required.
  • Phone listings, listing photos, product information, prices and related seller-provided content.
  • Booking and visit-related information.
  • Wishlist and other saved activity associated with your account.
  • Support requests and information you provide when contacting support.
  • Location information when you choose to use location-based features.
  • Notification tokens and information necessary to deliver notifications.
  • Technical, security and diagnostic information necessary to operate and protect the service.

2. Account and Authentication

BookMyPhone may use Firebase Authentication and supported sign-in methods such as email/password authentication and Google Sign-In. Authentication providers may process information necessary to verify your identity and maintain your signed-in session.

Passwords used for authentication are handled by the authentication provider. BookMyPhone does not intentionally store your authentication password as plain text.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Create and maintain customer and seller accounts.
  • Authenticate users and protect accounts.
  • Display and manage seller stores and phone listings.
  • Process and manage booking and store-visit workflows.
  • Maintain wishlists and relevant account activity.
  • Send booking, listing, seller, support and service notifications.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Review seller verification information where applicable.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, misuse and security incidents.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot and improve the reliability of the service.
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

4. Location Information

BookMyPhone may request access to location information for features that depend on location, such as helping users find relevant stores or obtain directions.

Location permission is subject to your device settings. You can deny or revoke location permission through your device settings. Some location-dependent features may not work correctly if permission is unavailable.

5. Seller Information and KYC

Sellers may be required to provide additional profile, store or verification information. Where seller verification or KYC is required, the information is used for verification, platform safety, fraud prevention and compliance purposes.

Verification does not constitute a guarantee of a seller, product, transaction or future conduct.

6. Listings, Photos and User-Provided Content

Sellers may provide listing information, photographs, store images and other content required to operate their store or listings. This information may be displayed to customers where appropriate for operation of the marketplace.

7. Bookings and Store Visits

When a customer books a phone or arranges a store visit through BookMyPhone, relevant booking information may be made available to the seller so the booking or visit can be managed.

BookMyPhone primarily facilitates discovery, booking and store visits. The condition and suitability of a pre-owned phone should be inspected by the customer before completing a purchase with the seller.

8. Payments and Promotional Services

Where payment functionality or optional paid services are available, payment processing may be handled by a third-party payment provider. BookMyPhone does not intentionally store complete payment card or banking credentials when those credentials are processed directly by the payment provider.

Information necessary to record payment status, transaction references, service eligibility or related records may be processed where required for the relevant feature.

9. Notifications

BookMyPhone may use notification services such as Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver important account and marketplace notifications, including booking updates, seller updates, listing-related information and support-related messages.

You may manage notification permissions through your device settings.

10. Service Providers and Infrastructure

BookMyPhone may use third-party service providers and infrastructure where necessary to operate the application. Depending on the feature being used, these may include services for:

  • Authentication and database functionality.
  • Cloud infrastructure and application security.
  • File and image storage.
  • Push notifications.
  • Maps, location or directions functionality.
  • Payment processing where applicable.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

11. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared only where reasonably necessary to operate BookMyPhone, such as between customers and sellers for marketplace functionality, with service providers supporting the application, for fraud or security investigations, or where disclosure is required by applicable law.

12. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no internet-connected service or electronic storage method can guarantee absolute security.

13. Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain legitimate business and security records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it is processed.

14. Account and Data Requests

Users may request assistance concerning their account or personal information through BookMyPhone's available account, privacy or support features.

Where applicable, users may request deletion of their account and associated personal information. Certain information may be retained where necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or legal compliance.

Privacy and account-related requests may also be sent to: bookmyphonehelp@gmail.com

If we become aware that information has been provided by a child contrary to applicable requirements, we may take appropriate steps to remove or restrict that information.

16. Analytics and Diagnostics

Where analytics or diagnostic services are configured, technical information may be processed to understand application performance, diagnose errors, improve reliability and protect the service.

BookMyPhone does not claim to use analytics services that have not actually been configured in the application.

17. Third-Party Services

BookMyPhone may contain functionality that interacts with third-party services, websites, maps, payment providers or external applications. Their handling of information is governed by their respective terms and privacy policies.

18. Your Choices

Depending on the feature and applicable law, you may have choices regarding your information, including managing device permissions, notification permissions, account information, and requesting access, correction or deletion of certain personal information.
