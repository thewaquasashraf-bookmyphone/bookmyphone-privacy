BookMyPhone Account Deletion

Last Updated: 13 September 2026

BookMyPhone users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

Delete Your Account from the BookMyPhone App

If you have access to your BookMyPhone account, use the account deletion option available inside the app.

Open BookMyPhone, sign in to your account, navigate to the relevant Profile or Settings area, and use the available account deletion option. Follow the confirmation steps shown in the app.

Request Account Deletion Without the App

If you cannot access the BookMyPhone app or your account, you can initiate an account deletion request by contacting BookMyPhone Support from the email address associated with your BookMyPhone account.

Send an email with the subject "BookMyPhone Account Deletion Request" to:

Request Account Deletion

Email: bookmyphonehelp@gmail.com

Information to Include

To help us identify the correct account, send the request from the email address associated with your BookMyPhone account where possible. We may request reasonable verification before processing a deletion request in order to protect accounts from unauthorized deletion.

Do not send passwords, OTPs, complete payment credentials, or other sensitive authentication secrets by email.

What Will Be Deleted

When an eligible account deletion request is completed, personal information associated with the account will be deleted or anonymized as applicable to the BookMyPhone service and the user's account type.

This may include account/profile information and other personal information associated with use of BookMyPhone, subject to information that must reasonably be retained for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, transaction or booking records, or applicable legal obligations.

Seller Accounts

Seller accounts may contain additional store, listing, booking, verification, support, or other marketplace records. Some records may need to be retained where required for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or legal purposes even after the seller account is deleted.
