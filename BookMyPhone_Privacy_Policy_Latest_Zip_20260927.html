This Privacy Policy explains how BookMyPhone handles personal data when Customers and Sellers use the platform.

BookMyPhone is currently operated as an individual/proprietor activity and is not incorporated as a company or LLP. Official and grievance contact details are available in the app Legal Information screen.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the account and features used, BookMyPhone may process name, email, mobile number, authentication identifiers, Customer/Seller role, profile information, Seller/store details, store address/location, business information, opening hours and media, Seller verification/KYC information and documents, listing data, booking/visit data, Wishlist activity, support requests, user-selected or permission-based location data, notification/device tokens, and technical/security/diagnostic information generated by configured services.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to authenticate users, maintain accounts, enforce Customer/Seller permissions, provide listings and search/discovery, process booking/visit requests, enable relevant Customer/Seller contact, display store information, operate Wishlist/catalog features, send service notifications, verify Sellers, prevent fraud/abuse, investigate reports, provide support, secure the platform, maintain operational records, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Authentication and Google Sign-In

BookMyPhone uses Firebase Authentication for account authentication and may offer Google Sign-In. Authentication providers may process identifiers and account information necessary to sign in and secure an account according to their applicable privacy terms.

4. Customer Contact Privacy

Customer phone numbers are not intended to be publicly displayed on marketplace listings. Relevant Customer contact information may be disclosed to the appropriate Seller in connection with a valid booking where required for the booking/contact flow and must not be exposed to unrelated Sellers.

5. Seller and Store Information

Public Seller/store information may include store name, logo, business description, public contact information, store location, opening hours, verification status, listings, and ratings/reviews where available. Seller KYC/verification records are treated as restricted verification information and are not intended for public display.

6. Seller KYC

Seller verification may involve identity/business details and supporting documents or photographs. KYC information is processed for Seller verification, platform safety, fraud prevention, compliance, and related support/administrative purposes. Access should be limited to authorized platform processes/personnel and service providers where necessary for those purposes.

7. Location

Users may select location manually. If a user chooses current-location functionality, device permission may be requested and location data may be processed to support discovery, distance, store-location, or visit functionality. Users may deny device location permission and use available manual-location features where supported.

8. Product and Store Media

Seller-uploaded product/store images may be stored through BookMyPhone's configured media infrastructure. Catalog/model imagery may come from permitted catalog/manufacturer sources and is separate from Seller-uploaded physical-device photographs.

9. Firebase, Cloudflare and Other Service Providers

BookMyPhone uses or may use configured services including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Cloudflare infrastructure/R2 object storage, maps/location providers, payment providers where a payment feature is used, and necessary technical service providers. These providers may process information on BookMyPhone's behalf or under their own applicable terms as required to deliver their services.

10. Notifications

FCM/device tokens may be processed to deliver booking, account, support, marketplace, and permitted service notifications. Device notification permissions can be controlled through operating-system settings. In-app notification/history records may remain where reasonably required for service, booking, security, support, or operational history.

11. Sharing

Personal information may be shared only as reasonably necessary with the relevant Customer/Seller for a booking, configured infrastructure/service providers, professional advisers where necessary, regulators/law-enforcement or competent authorities where legally required, or a lawful successor to the business. BookMyPhone does not sell Customer contact information as an unrelated marketing database.

12. Security

BookMyPhone uses technical and organizational safeguards intended to reduce unauthorized access or misuse, including authentication, authorization/security rules, Customer/Seller data separation, restricted booking/contact access, cloud infrastructure controls, and security/audit measures where configured. No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security.

13. Retention

Personal data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for active accounts, marketplace operation, booking/history, fraud/security, disputes, support, legal/compliance requirements, and legitimate operational purposes. Some Seller/listing/booking or security records may be retained or archived when necessary for those purposes even after an account or listing is no longer active.

14. Account and Data Requests

Subject to applicable law, users may request correction/update of applicable profile information, account deletion, access/information about personal data, withdrawal of consent where applicable, and grievance handling through available BookMyPhone account or Help & Support tools. Deletion does not require BookMyPhone to erase information that must or may lawfully be retained for fraud/security, disputes, legal obligations, or other permitted purposes.

15. Children

BookMyPhone is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older. BookMyPhone does not knowingly seek to provide marketplace accounts to children. If BookMyPhone becomes aware that a child's personal data has been processed contrary to applicable requirements, appropriate steps may be taken to restrict the account and address the data in accordance with applicable law.

16. Advertising, Analytics, Local Storage and Diagnostics

BookMyPhone may display Google AdMob advertising. Advertising SDKs may process device/app identifiers and related technical information for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention as configured. Consent choices and applicable privacy options are handled through Google's User Messaging Platform where required. Users should review the in-app privacy options when presented. The app may also use necessary local storage, security information, crash/diagnostic information, or analytics where configured for operation, reliability, security, or product improvement.

17. Third-Party Links and Maps

BookMyPhone may open external services such as map/navigation applications or external links. Once a user leaves BookMyPhone, the external service's own privacy practices apply.

18. Changes to this Privacy Policy

BookMyPhone may update this Privacy Policy as functionality or legal requirements change. Material changes may be communicated through the app or another appropriate channel.

19. Privacy / Grievance Requests

Privacy or grievance requests can be submitted through BookMyPhone Help & Support. Operator and grievance contact details are published in the Legal Information screen.

Contact email: bookmyphonehelp@gmail.com

20. External Account Deletion

Users can request account/data deletion in the app and through the BookMyPhone web deletion resource published with the app. Deletion remains subject to lawful retention described in this Policy.
